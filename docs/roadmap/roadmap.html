Everything is made by hand, stroke by stroke, and the fire is a living thing you feed.

Furnace
Clay Furnace · up to 800 C · 1 crucible
It is
Rubble and clay around a fire. It smelts tin and copper, forges bronze, and keeps the night away.
Material
fired clay, charcoal, bone, copper
Colour

Sound

  • First cup: a boiling pail, a cup, a drip cone; steam rises and a buff icon appears scripted by objectives and sound, never a cutscene
Goals in order40 objectives
  1. Gather wood gather_logsBreak four oak logs or four pine logs. Bare hands are slow, but they work. Wood is your first fuel and your first building material.
  2. Set up a chopping block place_chopping_blockStand a log on end: that is a Chopping Block (hand recipe, one log). Strike a log on it, stroke by stroke, to split planks. An axe in hand doubles every stroke.
  3. Split planks make_planksStrike a log on the chopping block, stroke by stroke: it splits into four planks (six with an axe in hand).
  4. Set up a workbench place_workbenchMake a workbench from six planks and put it down. It is only for cold work; its real job is to build your first furnace.
  5. Find flint find_flintDig gravel until you carry three flints. About one block in four hides one.
  6. Make a flint pickaxe make_pickaxeTwist fiber from leaves or tall grass into cord, strike a plank on the chopping block for sticks, and bind a flint pickaxe. Without one, stone gives you nothing.
  7. Quarry rubble collect_rubbleMine stone with the pickaxe until you carry twelve rubble for the furnace walls.
  8. Dig clay collect_clayDig six blocks of clay to line the furnace. It is most often found where land meets water.
  9. Build your furnace place_furnaceMake a Clay Furnace at the workbench and set it where you want your home to be. You return to your furnace when things go wrong, and everything you build will grow around it.
  10. Light the fire light_furnaceOpen the furnace, put sticks, planks or logs in the firebox and light it. Watch the temperature climb.
  11. Bake charcoal make_charcoalLoad logs into the crucible. Above 300 C they bake into charcoal, a fuel that burns at 1000 C.
  12. Warm yourself get_warmStay close to the furnace while it is above 400 C. After a few seconds you are Well-Warmed: you mine faster and the cold bites half as hard.
  13. Cook a meal cook_foodHunt a hen, goat or boar, or pull up a wild potato, and load it into a crucible. Above 200 C it cooks in seconds.
  14. Eat eat_foodHold the food and press Use. Cooked food fills you far more than raw, and a full stomach lets you heal.
  15. Build a clay table place_clay_tableMake a Clay Table at the workbench from two planks and a clay and put it down. Clay worked on it by hand becomes cups, pots and moulds.
  16. Fire a mould fire_mouldPress two clay into an ingot mould on the clay table, then load it into a crucible and fire it at 500 C. Metal is poured into moulds, so you need one before you smelt.
  17. Smelt copper smelt_copperCopper ore needs 700 C. Burn charcoal, or pump the bellows over a log fire, and smelt two ingots.
  18. Pour your first ingot first_pourSmelt copper, then hold the mould and take the molten metal from the crucible within 20 seconds. Metal taken without a mould cools into a rough bloom that must be smelted again.
  19. Set up a hand quern place_quernMake a Hand Quern at the workbench from two stone bricks and a stick. It grinds grain stroke by stroke.
  20. Till the soil till_soilBind a flint hoe from flint, sticks and cord, then use it on the top of grass or dirt to turn it into farmland.
  21. Plant wheat plant_seedsUse wheat seeds on farmland. Wheat grows in daylight, twice as fast with water within four blocks, and takes a few minutes: carry on with the furnace meanwhile. Tall grass and wild wheat give more see
  22. Grind flour grind_flourLoad wheat into the quern and use it until the work bar fills: one wheat, one flour.
  23. Bake bread bake_breadHarvest four ripe wheat and grind them into flour on the hand quern. Mix the flour with a bucket of water into dough, and bake it in a crucible at 220 C.
  24. Boil a bucket boil_bucketFill a bucket with water and set it on top of your hot furnace (aim at the top and Use). In a few seconds it boils and steams.
  25. Brew a cup first_brewForge a clay cup and a drip cone at 400 C, set the cone on the cup, load it with dried tea (tea leaves dried in a crucible), then pour boiling water over it.
  26. Taste them all six_cupsGreen, black and berry tea, light and dark roast, and trail blend each give a different boost. Brew and drink all six.
  27. Set up a stone anvil place_anvilMake a Stone Anvil at the workbench from three stone bricks. A flint hammer in hand doubles every stroke.
  28. Hammer a plate hammer_plateLoad two copper ingots on the Stone Anvil and strike until they are beaten flat. Metal fresh from the crucible is soft for 20 seconds and needs half the strokes.

  30. Forge bronze make_bronzeSmelt tin, set an Alloy Pot against the furnace (fire one from clay first), and melt three copper and one tin ingot into four bronze at 800 C, as hot as a Clay Furnace can get. Pour it into the ingot
  31. Cast a pickaxe head cast_pick_headFire a pickaxe mould, alloy three copper and one tin in an Alloy Pot set against the furnace at 800 C, and pour the molten bronze into the mould within 20 seconds.
  32. Forge a maul forge_maulThe old machines of the Old Works still walk at night. Cast a maul head, then bind it to two sticks with cord at the workbench: it digs nothing, but it breaks automaton plate.
  33. Forge a bronze pickaxe bronze_pickaxeBind the cast head to two sticks with cord at the workbench. A bronze pickaxe opens iron ore, sulfur and oil shale, and lasts three times as long as flint.

  35. Make electricity first_powerForge a Thermopile and set it against the furnace. It makes a quarter of a watt for every degree above 100 C, so a fire near 500 C gives 100 W.
  36. Light a lamp place_lampForge copper wire and a Lamp. Stand the lamp against the thermopile, or run cable between them, and it shines while the furnace is hot.
  37. Build a drying rack place_drying_rackLash three sticks and a cord into a Drying Rack and set it under open sky. It works by day and rain sets it back.
  38. Tan leather make_leatherSoak two hides for a minute in a Tanning Trough (four planks and a water bucket), then dry them on the rack in the sun.
  39. Weave cloth weave_clothMake a Loom at the workbench from four planks, two sticks and two cords, load four fiber and weave stroke by stroke. A padded coat is stuffed with cloth.
  40. Build the Bronze Furnace bronze_furnaceOpen the furnace screen and the upgrade list: eight bronze ingots, four firebrick and a bellows, with the fire at 700 C. Bronze plates round the firebox hold 1100 C and two crucibles. It ends the Embe

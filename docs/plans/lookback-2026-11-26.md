## Features that could have been

These are not in any plan, or only hinted at; each follows from what already exists.

1. **A middle that teaches the machines.** Steam was meant to automate the hand stations. Without it, a player goes from hand work straight to
   the Bronze workshop and then electricity, and electricity already starts in Ember (thermopile). A small steam loop (kettle, rotor, one
   axle, one automated hand station) would make the spine read as a story.
2. **A first-hour path built around the camp.** The camp start, Gruk, the lodge and the town are the newest and most personal part, but the
   objectives and story beats still follow ages only (`storyBeats.ts`). The first goals could be the camp's: light the fire, sleep a night,
   fill the first bounty, open the lodge.
3. **A balance and pacing check you can run.** The balance dossier has the numbers; nothing applies or tests them. A Dev Mode "time to each
   age" readout or a scripted first-hour bot would show where play stalls before anyone plays by hand.
4. **Dead-end items get a use.** Kerosene (lamp or tractor fuel), the free logs from `tendFire`, the three unused gear designs. Each is a
   recipe or one consumer away.
5. **Combat that grows.** Rarity, named finds and enchantments landed, but the parts of the RPG notes that give fights a goal (boss gating,
   monster-part crafting, affixes) did not. One boss at the end of the caves plan would tie loot, enchantments and caves together.
6. **The settlement as the main loop.** Contentment, housing and move-in are built and tested but unwired, so a new world never gets past
   Gruk. Wiring them turns the tavern, trader, festival and Bram back on and gives every other system a customer.

    The rules

    • Notes grow with the age: Ember radius 2, Bronze 3, Forge 4. Ink blots hold nothing.
    • Neighbours link only when one aspect is made from the other. An aspect never links to itself.
    • Beginning costs one lore of each fixed aspect. Each write costs one lore and one ink.
    • Erasing or overwriting gives nothing back. Hint shows one step of a solution with what you know.

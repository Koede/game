Hearthwatt Codex

Crafting map Blocks Progression Item map Essentia

Every recipe in the game, in the order you reach them: by hand, at the workbench, at the furnace tier by tier, then in machines. Furnace rows show the temperature the fire must reach. Tap any item to see what it is made from and what it goes into.

Every block in the world, with the tool that breaks it, the tier that tool must be for anything to drop, and what you get.

The whole game as one tree. Six ages. Each begins when the furnace reaches its next tier; the branches are what that tier opens up. Items shown as icons exist in the game today. Branches marked planned are designed but not built yet.

Every item placed by complexity: columns count the crafting steps from raw materials, rows are the furnace tier it needs in the game as built today (six tiers: Clay, Bronze, Blast, Arc, Plasma, Star). Tap an item to light up everything it requires (amber) and everything that needs it (cyan). The number is how many raw materials one of it costs in total.

The optional magic: every block and item has a nature made of aspects. Note things with the Field Notebook to learn them, link aspects on research boards to unlock recipes, gather essentia with a rod, and spend it at the Rune Desk and the Infusion Altar. The tech tree never needs any of it.
